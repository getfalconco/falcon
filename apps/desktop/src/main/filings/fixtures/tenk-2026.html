ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Acme designs sensors. Sales to Globex Corporation and Hooli Inc. accounted for 24% and 11% of our net revenue, respectively, for the year ended December 31, 2025.

We entered into a supply agreement with Umbrella Materials for wafers. We also work with Initech for machined housings.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The risks described below are not the only ones we face.

Risks Related to Our Business

Our revenue depends on a small number of customers, and the loss of any of them could harm our results.

Sales to our largest customers are concentrated. We rely on a single source supplier for our image sensor dies, and any interruption could delay shipments.

We depend on Umbrella Materials as the sole source of our wafers. Umbrella Materials is our sole supplier of 200mm wafers and we have no second source qualified.

Our operating results fluctuate from quarter to quarter, which makes them difficult to predict.

Our results have varied in the past.

Climate change and extreme weather may have a long-term impact on our business and our suppliers.

Extreme weather affects our facilities.

Risks Related to Our Common Stock

The market price of our common stock in fiscal 2025 has been volatile and may continue to be volatile.

Our stock price has moved widely.

We have identified a material weakness in our internal control over financial reporting.

See Item 9A.
