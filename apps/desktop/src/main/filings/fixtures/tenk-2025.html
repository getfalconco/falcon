ITEM 1.  BUSINESS
Acme designs sensors. Sales to Globex Corporation accounted for 18% of our net revenue in 2024, 15% in 2023 and 12% in 2022.
We work with many suppliers, including Initech for machined housings.
ITEM 1A.    RISK FACTORS
The risks described below are not the only ones we face.
Risks Related to Our Business
Our revenue depends on a small number of customers, and the loss of any of them could harm our results.
Sales to our largest customers are concentrated. We rely on a single source supplier for our image sensor dies, and any interruption could delay shipments.
Our operating results fluctuate from quarter to quarter, which makes them difficult to predict.
Our results have varied in the past.
Climate change may have a long-term impact on our business.
Extreme weather affects our facilities.
Risks Related to Our Common Stock
The market price of our common stock in fiscal 2024 has been volatile and may continue to be volatile.
Our stock price has moved widely.
We have never paid dividends on our common stock.
We do not plan to.
